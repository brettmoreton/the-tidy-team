Who are The Tidy Team?

We're a group of parents, governors, and local volunteers who care about Ysgol Llandrillo Yn Rhos — and want it to look its best.

The school is a brilliant place. But keeping the site in good shape takes more than the staff can manage on their own. So we show up on the first Sunday of every month, roll our sleeves up, and get things done — painting, gardening, tidying, fixing.

No experience needed. No commitment required. Just turn up when you can.

We're always looking for more hands. The more people who join us, the more we can do — and the better the school looks for the kids who spend their days there.

Our story so far

How we got here

The Tidy Team grew out of a series of Community Gardening Days held at the school since late 2024. Each one brought together pupils, parents, families and friends from across the local community. Together we've cleared, planted, tidied and slowly transformed the grounds — and built something that's outgrown the one-off format. From June 2026 onwards, we're going monthly.
